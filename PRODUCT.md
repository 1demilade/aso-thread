# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite, JavaScript and plain CSS (user-specified). Client-side routing, React state for filters, selections and the order bag. No backend.

## Users

- Primary: a prospective fashion-business client reviewing this as a portfolio demonstration; they judge whether it feels like a real premium label.
- In-world: shoppers in Nigeria and the diaspora browsing ready-to-wear, contemporary African and traditional Nigerian outfits on phone first, then ordering by WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Website for **Aso & Thread**, a fictional Nigerian fashion label (ready-to-wear, contemporary African fashion, traditional Nigerian outfits). Visitors browse collections, choose size and colour, and send a pre-filled WhatsApp order. Success: the site reads as a confident, premium independent label and every interaction works.

## Positioning

A Lagos label (fictional studio in Yaba) that cuts Nigerian cloth (aso-oke, adire, ankara, lace) into both everyday ready-to-wear and occasion traditional dress. "Aso" is Yoruba for cloth.

## Operating Context

- Ordering happens through WhatsApp chat, not checkout: the message carries product, size, colour, quantity and price in naira (₦).
- WhatsApp number is a clearly marked placeholder (`234XXXXXXXXXX`), never a real number.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five pages: Home, Collections (filterable grid), Product Details (reusable per product), About, Contact.
- Contact form: front-end validation only; must not claim a message was sent.
- No payment gateway.

## Brand Commitments

- Modern, confident, premium, recognisably African; no clichéd decorative patterns, no generic e-commerce template look.

## Evidence on Hand

- Imagery: Unsplash photography (user-approved), downloaded and credited; stands in for future campaign shoots.
- None: no real customers, testimonials, press, partnerships, stockists or business statistics. Never invent them. This is a fictional portfolio concept.
- Product names, descriptions and prices are sample content.

## Product Principles

1. Cloth first: the fabric and its origin carry the story.
2. Honest commerce: what you select is exactly what the WhatsApp message says.
3. Phone first: most visitors arrive from social on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard-operable selectors and filters, visible focus, reduced-motion support.
